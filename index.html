The Project

Engineering a New Perspective on Energy

AEROVIBE-X explores the recovery of small amounts of energy from acoustic excitation and mechanical vibrations using piezoelectric discs and electronic measurement circuits.

The project is centered on practical investigation: applying controlled inputs, observing electrical response, and documenting what the prototype can demonstrate under real experimental conditions.

System Overview

How It Works

A clear path from mechanical excitation to measured data. Actual behavior depends on the circuit, materials, and test conditions.

Energy Input

Sound-induced or mechanical vibrations provide the experimental input.

Piezoelectric Conversion

Piezoelectric discs generate electrical charge when mechanically stressed.

Electrical Conditioning

Rectification and suitable conditioning prepare output for measurement.

Measurement

Sensors measure relevant parameters; a microcontroller processes readings.

Data Display

An OLED can present measured values where supported by the hardware setup.

Energy Storage

Rated energy-management circuitry may store a portion of harvested energy.
